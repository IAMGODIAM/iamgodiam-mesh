What this paper teaches

The paper begins with identity: the Eternal Son is the original and only-begotten Son of God, associate creator of all things, and the Father's full equal — his perfect complement and eternal counterpart. He is just as changeless and infinitely dependable as the Father, and he shares the Trinity's justice and righteousness, but overshadows those sterner traits by the infinite personalization of the Father's love and mercy; he is the revelation of divine love to the universes. His domain is spirit: he motivates the spirit level of cosmic reality, while in the physical and mind domains he works through others rather than in person.

Why it matters

For people told that ultimate authority is distant, armed, and indifferent, this paper's Son is a rebuke. The Father's own self-expression — his Word — turns out to be a personality in whom love and mercy overshadow justice, not a decree that merely terrifies. God does not stay unexpressed at the center of things; he speaks himself outward in a Son who can be known, and through whom the Father's character becomes legible to creatures. Power at its highest, in this telling, does not hide. It reveals itself, personally, as love — and that makes the lowliest knower a legitimate reader of God.
