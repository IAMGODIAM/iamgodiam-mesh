How is our world numbered in the government of the universe?

Paper 43 gives our world an address. Urantia is commonly referred to as 606 of Satania in Norlatiadek of Nebadon: the six hundred sixth inhabited world of the local system of Satania, within the constellation of Norlatiadek, one of the one hundred constellations of the local universe of Nebadon. Constellations are the primary divisions of a local universe, each administered under its own legislative enactments, and their rulers link the local systems upward to Salvington and, by reflectivity, to the Ancients of Days on Uversa.

What this paper teaches

The paper describes the constellation government seated in a cluster of 771 architectural spheres centered on Edentia, seat of the Constellation Fathers, the Most Highs of Norlatiadek — rulers of the Vorondadek order of sonship. Edentia abounds in highlands and sparkling lakes, crowned with morontia life, though without Urantia's rugged mountain ranges. The most holy mount of assembly houses the Faithful of Days, the Paradise Trinity's representative. The paper notes that the rotation of the Most Highs was suspended at the time of the Lucifer rebellion, introduces the univitatia, the permanent citizens supervising all 770 surrounding worlds, and ends with ascending mortals who, after graduation from world number seventy, take up residence on Edentia.

Why it matters

To be numbered 606 might sound like being lost in a census, but the paper means the opposite: the smallest inhabited world has a known place in an ordered commonwealth, and its affairs connect, link by link, to the highest councils. Constellations govern themselves under their own legislative enactments — local self-rule is built into the architecture of heaven, not tolerated as an exception. Even the suspension of the Most Highs' rotation in rebellion days did not erase Norlatiadek's standing. A world can pass through disloyalty at the top and still be held, named, and expected at the capital.
