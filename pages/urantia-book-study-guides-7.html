What this paper teaches

The paper maps the Son's work across three reaches. On Paradise, his presence and personal activity are profound and absolute in the spiritual sense. Out in the local universes, ascending mortals meet him through the Creator Sons, who serve as his personal representatives. And across everything runs his everlasting liaison with the Father in one shared project, named here as the divine plan of progress: the creation, evolution, ascension, and perfection of will creatures. It was the Son who joined the Father in broadcasting the invitation-command “Be you perfect, even as your Father in Havona is perfect” — and the paper warns that ignorance of the many Sons of God is a source of great confusion on Urantia.

Why it matters

Here is the paper's tension, and its dignity: the Son's grasp of spirit realities is absolute — his spirit gravity never fails — yet he is not responsible for the conduct of freewill personalities, because the creature's will is relatively free and determines its own actions. The deepest order in the universe, in other words, holds persons without crushing their agency. Nobody is a puppet of heaven. And the standing invitation of that order — “Be you perfect” — was issued jointly by Father and Son to all creation: the call to grow belongs to every will creature, addressed to each one by name.
