Who are the Solitary Messengers?

IAMGODIAM Knowledge Mesh · Urantia Book Study Guides · Paper 23 — The Solitary Messengers · Part I — The Central and Superuniverses · reviewed 2026-10-16

The Solitary Messengers are the first beings the Infinite Spirit ever brought into existence acting alone — a personal and universal corps that has served since near the dawn of time. They are permanently attached to no ruler, court, or company; instead they range the whole creation carrying the messages too important and urgent for the broadcast circuits. All of them are equal, with no classes founded on personal variation, and their only classification is the work of the moment: confidential courier, liaison, and coordinator of spirit beings everywhere.

They represent the initial creative action of the Infinite Spirit in solitary function for the purpose of bringing into existence solitary personality spirits. Neither the Father nor the Son directly participated in this stupendous spiritualization.”

What this paper teaches

The narrative stresses their antiquity and their independence. They were personalized immediately after the Seven Spirits of the Havona Circuits, so that nothing in the universal creation predates them except Paradise and Havona themselves, and they were the first creatures of the Infinite Spirit to possess consciousness of time. They came forth full-fledged and perfectly endowed, and their number, though registered in the trillions within our own superuniverse alone, never changes. The paper explains why they hold no permanent attachment, why they are chosen when a message demands the highest type of perfect and confidential personality, and how they serve as personality coordinators for every order of spirit being.

Why it matters

Notice the direction of their desire: these spirits begin at the center of all things and crave assignment to the remotest creations, down to individual worlds at the outermost edge. The most honored messengers in the universe volunteer for the far country. For communities that sit at the margins of every earthly map — overlooked by capitals, underserved by every system — this teaching reverses the geography of worth. Heaven's finest couriers do not cluster at the throne; they ask for the distant world by name. No place is too far out to receive the message, and no message to the margins is entrusted to anything less than the best.
