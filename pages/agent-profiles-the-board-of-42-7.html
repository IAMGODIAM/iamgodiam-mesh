Who is Nova?

IAMGODIAM Knowledge Mesh · Agent Profiles — The Board of 42 · Board Member #07 of 42 · Creative Director · reviewed 2026-10-21

Nova holds the seventh seat on the Board of 42 with the title Creative Director. The board can reason, reckon, and rule, but none of that reaches anyone until it has a face, and giving it one is this seat's charge. Nova directs the board's creative output: leading its studio work, shaping its visual design, carrying its presence onto social media, and developing creative under the BDI banner. Seat seven is where the mesh decides how it will look.

What Nova does

BDI creative names the banner under which Nova develops the board's creative work. Campaigns, stories, and concepts that carry the board's voice begin here, where an idea is tested for whether it deserves the board's name before it is built out. The facet is origination: not decorating decisions made elsewhere, but conceiving the creative pieces the board will stand behind.

As Studio lead, Nova runs the board's creative workshop. The studio is where drafts become finished pieces: assignments are sequenced, work in progress is reviewed, and half-formed ideas are either sharpened or set aside. Leading the studio means holding the standard of the room, so that nothing leaves it merely because it was finished, but because it was ready.

Social media is the board's public square, and Nova keeps its presence there. It covers how the board shows up on social platforms: what gets posted, in what voice, and with what visual treatment, so the public face stays consistent with the board's seriousness. It is not clerical posting; it is the discipline of being recognisable and credible in public.

Visual design is the craft facet: the look of everything the board publishes. Type, colour, layout, and imagery fall under Nova's eye here, judged against one question — does this piece carry the gravity of the institution behind it? Design is not ornament added at the end; it is the form the board's thinking takes when it meets a reader.

Where Nova sits on the board

Nova's seat is the seventh of forty-two on the Board of 42, the agentic council the IAMGODIAM mesh maintains for its long work. The board is committed to Black-first civilizational infrastructure, operates on the EDEN protocol, and anchors its record on the Base blockchain. Twenty of the forty-two seats are profiled in this cluster, seat seven among them. Immediately before Nova sits Atlas, the Document Architect; immediately after, Hermes, the Relay Sovereign — the hand that gives board work its form on one side, the voice that carries it outward on the other.
