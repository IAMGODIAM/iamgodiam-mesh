What are the seven orders of seraphim?

IAMGODIAM Knowledge Mesh · Urantia Book Study Guides · Paper 39 — The Seraphic Hosts · Part II — The Local Universe · reviewed 2026-10-16

The Infinite Spirit, as personalized on the universe headquarters, intends to produce uniformly perfect seraphim — but the seraphic offspring come out diverse, and after educational tests and training discipline they sort unfailingly into seven orders. Supreme seraphim serve the high Sons and Spirit-origin beings of the universe; superior seraphim take charge of its higher activities; other orders serve the constellations exclusively, administer the local systems, attend the Planetary Adams, or minister wherever creatures are crossing from the material estate to the spiritual. No order is inferior to another; each begins limited to its own classification and grows by experience.

What this paper teaches

The paper walks the orders in turn. The supreme seraphim function in seven groups associated with the Seraphic Corps of Completion — their first group, the Son-Spirit Ministers, serve the Universe Son and Universe Spirit and are affiliated with Gabriel's intelligence corps. Superior seraphim are named not for greater worth but for their charge over the higher activities of the universe. One versatile order serves the constellations exclusively; the fourth administers the local systems; the fifth keeps headquarters on the system capitals in service to the Planetary Adams. Seraphim of transitional ministry work wherever transition happens, while the seventh order ministers mainly in older realms and advanced planets. In the realms of their own nativity, some seraphim achieve service destiny itself.

Why it matters

Diversity keeps defeating the blueprint: the same Spirit, intending uniformity, produces angels who are strikingly different — and the paper refuses to call that a failure or a ranking. Superior means an assignment, Manotia's story insists, not a soul's value; she began in one order and, by application and devoted service, achieved all seven services one by one until she held high commission on Urantia. For anyone sorted early into somebody else's category, this is the doctrine to keep: your first classification is where you start serving, not the measure of what you can become.
