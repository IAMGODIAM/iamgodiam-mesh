Paper 116 presents the Almighty Supreme as a living, evolving Deity of power and personality — the God of time and space whose present experience includes growth and incomplete status. What this paper teaches

The paper unfolds the Almighty's growth. His power presence appears alongside the high creators and controllers of the evolutionary superuniverses; the Paradise Deities act through agencies and manifestations, including God the Sevenfold, who encompasses creators and controllers alike. The unity of the Supreme Whole depends on the progressive unification of the finite parts, so the Almighty's actualization is both result and producer of those unifications. In the superuniverses, energy-matter dominates except in personality, where spirit through mind struggles for mastery. The grand universe itself, the paper concludes, is a magnificent and responsive living organism — not a machine.

Why it matters

This paper refuses to let faith be used as a sedative. If people recognized the God of time and space as evolving and nonabsolute, it says, faith would no longer be prostituted to the smugness of the fortunate or to stoical resignation in the victims of social deprivation. Read plainly: a growing God does not bless the arrangement that keeps some comfortable and others crushed. Inequality belongs to the unfinished state of things, not to heaven's design — and partnership with a growing God means working on what is still incomplete.
