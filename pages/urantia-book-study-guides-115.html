What this paper teaches

The paper first examines the frames every mind builds in order to think — indispensable, it concedes, yet erroneous to a greater or lesser degree. It distinguishes the existential standpoint, in which nothing new can happen, from the experiential, in which new realities genuinely emerge as potentials become actual. It treats the absolute cosmos as conceptually without limit, insists that any account of the Supreme's origins must begin with the Paradise Trinity, and states plainly that the Supreme Being depends on the Trinity for his personal and spirit nature. It closes with the Father-I AM finding liberation in the Supreme from the limitations of infinity, eternity, and absoluteness.

Why it matters

With God the Supreme, the paper says, achievement is the prerequisite to status — one must do something as well as be something. Sit with that beside every order that assigns worth at birth: bloodline, caste, pedigree, zip code. Here is a Deity whose own standing is bound up with doing, growing, and becoming — a God who does not merely inherit eternity but achieves actuality through experience shared with creatures. A people long denied status can recognize the pattern: dignity demonstrated in the doing, and a universe that finally honors it.
