How does a Thought Adjuster work with one person's life?

IAMGODIAM Knowledge Mesh · Urantia Book Study Guides · Paper 110 — Relation of Adjusters to Individual Mortals · Part III — The History of Urantia · reviewed 2026-10-16

From within the mind, not the brain. The Adjuster indwells the mortal mind rather than any physical organ, bringing with it the model career, the ideal life, certified on Divinington, and it works through a person's successive conquest of the seven psychic circles of mortal potentiality. It is not conscience, the paper warns, and its achievement is measured not by circle attainment alone but by the growing likeness of a life to its divine pattern.

“THE endowment of imperfect beings with freedom entails inevitable tragedy, and it is the nature of the perfect ancestral Deity to universally and affectionately share these sufferings in loving companionship.”

What this paper teaches

The paper gets practical about the partnership. Adjusters bring with them the model careers, the ideal lives, foreordained by themselves and the Personalized Adjusters of Divinington — a pattern for this particular life. They are in full touch with the spirit intelligence of the universes, receiving the continuous stream of cosmic intelligence over the master circuits of time and space, yet the paper insists their mission not be confounded with conscience; the two are not directly related. Personality realization on a material world is contained in the successive conquest of the seven psychic circles of mortal potentiality — though achieving all seven circles, it cautions, does not equal Adjuster fusion. The Adjusters, it says, are playing the sacred and superb game of the ages.

Why it matters

The opening line of this paper may be its most pastoral: because God endowed imperfect beings with freedom, tragedy is inevitable — and it is the nature of Deity to share those sufferings in loving companionship. God is not spectating. The indweller of this paper is also, we are told, deeply interested in your temporal welfare — delighted to contribute to your health, happiness, and true prosperity, concerned with your daily doings wherever they shape your significant choices. For people taught to spiritualize away their material conditions, that is a correction: the divine partner honors the daily struggle itself as the arena where eternity is decided.
