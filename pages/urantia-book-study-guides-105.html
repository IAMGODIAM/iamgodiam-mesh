What this paper teaches

The paper builds carefully from a single postulate. Picture eternity-infinity, it suggests, as an almost limitless ellipse produced by one absolute cause, forever diversifying toward some infinite potential of destiny. All absolute reality is from eternity and without beginning of existence. Within the I AM, seven prime relationships eternalize as the Seven Absolutes of Infinity. The original diversification of the I AM is attributed to its inherent, self-contained volition, while the promulgation of finite reality is ascribed to the volitional acts of Paradise Deity — a transference from potentials to actuals within the associations of functional infinity. Beyond the finite it places the transcendentals: subinfinite and subabsolute, yet superfinite and supercreatural.

Why it matters

This paper is a charter for intellectual humility — and for intellectual courage. If even high universe intelligences comprehend infinity only partially, then no scholar, pulpit, or power on earth can claim to have reality finished and filed. Partial knowledge is not failed knowledge; it is the honest condition of every finite mind. That matters for people whose questions about God and the cosmos have been treated as presumption. Here the revelation itself begins by admitting the mystery is bigger than every mind that approaches it, and then thinks as boldly as it can anyway. Reverence and rigor belong together; neither cancels the other.
