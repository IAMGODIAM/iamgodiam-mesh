Who were Andon and Fonta, the first human beings?

IAMGODIAM Knowledge Mesh · Urantia Book Study Guides · Paper 63 — The First Human Family · Part III — The History of Urantia · reviewed 2026-10-16

Urantia was registered as an inhabited world because of two children: the twins Andon and Fonta, the first true human beings. Paper 63 tells their story — their decision to flee northward from their tribe, the birth of their children, the spread of their Andonite descendants, and the long retrogression of the clans until Onagar arose to lead them. It names this pair, without qualification, the actual parents of all mankind.

What this paper teaches

The paper opens with the archangel message from Salvington announcing that man-mind had appeared on 606 of Satania, and explains the names Andon and Fonta — “the first Fatherlike creature” and “the first Sonlike creature” to hunger for human perfection. It describes the twins’ parents, among the more intelligent of their tribe, the first to throw stones and fight with clubs; Andon’s flint-tipped weapon; the twins’ flight and their fears; their children and grandchildren; and the Andonites themselves — black-eyed, swarthy, a cross between yellow and red — whose races at first entered neither Asia deeply nor Africa at all.

Why it matters

Every human family on earth, this paper says, traces back to one family — a brother and sister who named themselves “loved by mother” and “loved by father.” That is a radical charter for human dignity. The first humans were not kings or priests; they were runaways who feared displeasing their parents and fled into the cold. And heaven’s response to their appearance was not suspicion but prayer — the archangels prayed that these creatures would receive the spirit of the Father. Our whole species begins, in this account, as somebody’s beloved children.
