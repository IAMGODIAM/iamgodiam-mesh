Who are the ministering spirits of the superuniverses?

IAMGODIAM Knowledge Mesh · Urantia Book Study Guides · Paper 28 — Ministering Spirits of the Superuniverses · Part I — The Central and Superuniverses · reviewed 2026-10-16

As the supernaphim are the angelic hosts of the central universe and the seraphim of the local universes, so the seconaphim are the ministering spirits of the superuniverses — children of the Reflective Spirits, much more like supernaphim than seraphim in divinity and supremacy. The paper presents their orders and assignments, and introduces their two associate families: the tertiaphim, the Infinite Spirit's gift of companion spirits to each Creator Son, and the omniaphim, the exclusive servants and messengers of the Seven Supreme Executives.

“AS THE supernaphim are the angelic hosts of the central universe and the seraphim of the local universes, so are the seconaphim the ministering spirits of the superuniverses. In degree of divinity and in potential of supremacy, however, these children of the Reflective Spirits are much more like supernaphim than seraphim. They serve not alone in the supercreations, and both numerous and intriguing are the transactions sponsored by their unrevealed associates.”

“Omniaphim are created by the Infinite Spirit in liaison with the Seven Supreme Executives, and they are the exclusive servants and messengers of these same Supreme Executives. Omniaphim are of grand universe assignment, and in Orvonton their corps maintains headquarters in the northerly parts of Uversa, where they reside as a special courtesy colony. What this paper teaches

The secoraphic hosts are produced by the seven Reflective Spirits stationed at the headquarters of each superuniverse, and they are created serially in seven reflective types. The primary seconaphim, assigned to the Ancients of Days, serve as living mirrors in the presence of those triune rulers; the secondary order is no less reflective, and the tertiary types are distributed across the separate services of the superuniverse administrators. With their liaison fellows, the seconaphim range from the shores of Paradise to the evolutionary worlds of space. The tertiaphim are personalized on Paradise in groups of one thousand whenever a Michael Son departs for his universe adventure, while the omniaphim devote themselves wholly to administrative coordination for the Supreme Executives.

Why it matters

A living mirror is a particular kind of servant: one whose gift is to show things exactly as they are. The rulers of the superuniverses keep such beings beside them — not flatterers, not spies, but mirrors. Every community knows how rare and costly honest reflection is, and how power prefers praise. This paper builds truth-telling into the furniture of heaven's government and honors the ones who carry it. And these same ministers range all the way down to the evolutionary worlds: the far, struggling planets are inside the circuit of their service, reflected truly and reported faithfully at the highest courts.
