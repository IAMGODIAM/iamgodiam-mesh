What Proof does

Platform quality assurance means Proof tests the platform the board works through, hunting for the flaws that would undermine every seat's labor at once. A defect in the shared platform multiplies across all who use it, so this duty guards the common ground itself — confirming that the board's house is sound before judging rooms within it.

Link verification is Proof's care for the board's connective tissue. Every link is a promise that something waits at the other end, and this duty checks those promises — following references to confirm they land where they claim to land. Dead or misdirected links quietly tax every reader, so Proof treats each connection as a claim requiring evidence.

Artifact QA extends the same discipline to everything the board produces. Documents, pages, and finished works pass under Proof's eye before they stand as the board's output, checked against what they purport to be. The duty protects the board's credibility: an artifact that reaches the world bearing the board's name must first bear Proof's scrutiny.
