Who are the Paradise Sons of God?

The Paradise Sons of God are the descending Sons brought into being at the center: Creator Sons from the Father and the Son, Magisterial Sons from the Son and the Spirit, and Trinity Teacher Sons from all three Persons together. Paper 20 classifies these orders and their ministries — creating and ruling local universes, sitting as magistrates of the realms, incarnating in bestowal, and teaching — the divine sonship that ranges from the eternal Isle to the inhabited worlds of time and space.

What this paper teaches

The paper works through the orders by their service. Creator Sons head the list; then come the Magisterial Sons, the Avonals, high magistrates of the realms who adjudicate the successive dispensations of the worlds of time — an inhabited world is usually visited by an Avonal on a magisterial mission before a bestowal Son appears. The mystery of how a Paradise Son becomes ready for mortal incarnation is frankly confessed as universal, guarded on Sonarington. Last come the Trinity Teacher Sons, brought into being by the Trinity itself, who volunteer for service whenever a world's progress shows the time ripe to initiate a spiritual age.

Why it matters

The direction of sonship in this paper is downward, and that is its good news. The descending Sons are dedicated to ministry on the worlds of time and space, facilitating the Paradise climb of the lowly creatures of evolutionary origin — the ascending sons of God. Heaven's highest orders reveal themselves not as distant sovereigns but as ministers: creators, servers, bestowers, judges, teachers, and truth revealers. For people who have only known power that extracts, this is a rival picture of greatness: the divine Sons spend themselves winning planets, one by one, to willing recognition — by service and bestowal, never by force.
