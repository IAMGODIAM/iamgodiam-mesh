What this paper teaches

The paper first corrects the old assignment: since Michael's bestowal, seraphim are assigned by human intelligence, spirituality, and destiny — the so-called subnormal have their own corps, and normal minds are grouped in seven classes by their attainment of the circles of progress. Why it matters

There is pastoral weight in a heaven that assigns guardians by destiny rather than by bloodline. The paper's own history admits an older, race-based assignment — and says it was superseded. What replaced it is attainment and need: even those the world labels least capable receive a dedicated corps, with justice and mercy witnessed on their behalf. For families who have prayed over children the systems wrote off, this teaching dignifies both the praying and the child: no one is unaccompanied, and no one's guardian is an accident of ancestry.
