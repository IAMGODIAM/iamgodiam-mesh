Who are the angels of the local universe?

IAMGODIAM Knowledge Mesh · Urantia Book Study Guides · Paper 38 — Ministering Spirits of the Local Universe · Part II — The Local Universe · reviewed 2026-10-16

Angels are the ministering spirits of time. In a local universe the angelic corps is made up of the seraphim, created by the Universe Mother Spirit in unit formations of 41,472 at a time, together with their close kin the cherubim and sanobim. Angels have no material bodies, yet they are definite and discrete beings, of spirit nature and origin. And they are still being created, periodically, because the universe of Nebadon is still in the making — still growing, still perfecting, still needing more ministers for more worlds.

“THERE are three distinct orders of the personalities of the Infinite Spirit. The impetuous apostle understood this when he wrote respecting Jesus, “who has gone to heaven and is on the right hand of God, angels and authorities and powers being made subject to him.” Angels are the ministering spirits of time; authorities, the messenger hosts of space; powers, the higher personalities of the Infinite Spirit.”

“The seraphim are all fairly uniform in design. From universe to universe, throughout all seven of the superuniverses, they show a minimum of variation; they are the most nearly standard of all spirit types of personal beings. Their various orders constitute the corps of the skilled and common ministers of the local creations.”

What this paper teaches

The paper follows a seraph's formation. Before commissioning, seraphim spend their first millennium as noncommissioned observers on Salvington and its associated world schools; after a second millennium at seraphic headquarters they are organized under chiefs into groups of twelve pairs, and twelve such groups make a company of 288 seraphim. The ninth group of seven primary spheres in the Salvington circuit are the seraphim's own worlds. Some orders of spirit beings function throughout the universe unrevealed, unconnected with the Paradise ascension plan. Cherubim and sanobim are similar to seraphim in all essential endowments, and numerous avenues of advancing service stand open to them, leading to enhancement of status — still further augmented by the embrace of the Divine Minister.

Why it matters

Heaven's angels, in this paper, are not ornaments around a throne; they are workers with a training pipeline two thousand years long, who begin as observers before they are trusted with a single charge. Even their beginnings are communal — created in companies, organized in pairs, never sent out alone. And the lowest orders, the cherubim, hold open avenues of advancement that can raise their status entirely. A kingdom where ministry is the highest identity, where the least angel can rise, and where nobody serves solo: that is a social vision worth carrying back down to earth.
