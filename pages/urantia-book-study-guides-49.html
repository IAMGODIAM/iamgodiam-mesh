What are the inhabited worlds like?

IAMGODIAM Knowledge Mesh · Urantia Book Study Guides · Paper 49 — The Inhabited Worlds · Part II — The Local Universe · reviewed 2026-10-16

Paper 49 surveys the mortal-inhabited worlds as a class. All of them are evolutionary in origin and nature — the spawning ground, the evolutionary cradle, of the mortal races of time and space — and each stage of the ascendant life is a training school for the stage just ahead. Satania itself is an unfinished system of only 619 inhabited worlds, on which Urantia holds the number 606. The paper ranges over how worlds are grouped, which planets can harbor life, the types of mortals who people them, and the ministry every one of them receives.

“Satania itself is an unfinished system containing only 619 inhabited worlds. Such planets are numbered serially in accordance with their registration as inhabited worlds, as worlds inhabited by will creatures. Thus was Urantia given the number 606 of Satania, meaning the 606th world in this local system on which the long evolutionary life process culminated in the appearance of human beings. There are thirty-six uninhabited planets nearing the life-endowment stage, and several are now being made ready for the Life Carriers. There are nearly two hundred spheres which are evolving so as to be ready for life implantation within the next few million years.”

What this paper teaches

The paper is specific about our system's census: Satania's 619 inhabited worlds include Anova, world number one, and some thirty-six uninhabited planets nearing the life-endowment stage, with nearly two hundred more evolving toward readiness for life implantation. Not all planets suit mortal life — small worlds with rapid axial spin are unsuited, and oversized planets crush with oppressive gravity, though their moons are often nearly ideal. There is a standard and basic pattern of vegetable and animal life in each system, most planets are peopled with the breathing type of intelligent beings, and mortals differ greatly from world to world — yet all mortals of will dignity are erect animals, bipeds. Every type alike enjoys the ministry of Thought Adjusters, guardian angels, and the messenger hosts of the Infinite Spirit.

Why it matters

Read the census again and notice the equalizer: worlds differ in size, age, type, and readiness, and the ministry does not. Thought Adjusters, guardian angels, and the hosts of the Infinite Spirit attend the newest world and the oldest alike — no planet is a second-class creation, and no people on any planet is born outside the care of heaven. The paper also refuses to rank the races of a world by their variations; difference is the standard pattern, not a defect. Whatever separates human beings from one another here, will dignity belongs to every mortal who has it — and every mortal has it.
