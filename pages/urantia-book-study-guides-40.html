Who are the Ascending Sons of God?

IAMGODIAM Knowledge Mesh · Urantia Book Study Guides · Paper 40 — The Ascending Sons of God · Part II — The Local Universe · reviewed 2026-10-16

Sonship is not reserved for one kind of creature. Seven classes of the Ascending Sons of God have been revealed, and mortals of animal origin are only one company among them. Guardian seraphim who serve ascending mortals can themselves ascend, attaining Paradise through Seraphington and even entering the Corps of Mortal Finality; the Material Sons and Daughters, though counted among the descending Sons, belong to the ascending story too. And the narrative that began with the Deities descends the whole scale of living beings until it reaches the lowest order of life endowed with the potential of immortality — us.

“The story of these beings, from the lowly animal-origin mortals of the evolutionary worlds to the Personalized Adjusters of the Universal Father, presents a glorious recital of the unstinted bestowal of divine love and gracious condescension throughout all time and in all universes of the far-flung creation of the Paradise Deities.”

What this paper teaches

The paper first gives the nonmortal ascending orders — seraphic, Adamic, midwayer, and Adjuster — before turning to the three basic orders of ascending mortals. Mortals, it says, represent the last link in the chain of those beings called sons of God; the mortal races stand as the lowest order of intelligent and personal creation. The sending and indwelling of the Adjusters is called one of the unfathomable mysteries of the Father. Practically all surviving mortals fuse with their Adjusters on the mansion worlds or the higher morontia spheres, though some fusions are delayed. The destinies differ by fusion: Spirit-fused mortals are generally confined to a local universe, Son-fused survivors to a superuniverse, while Adjuster-fused mortals are destined to penetrate the universe of universes.

Why it matters

The chain of sonship ends, deliberately, at the bottom. The lowest order of intelligent creation — mortal races on evolutionary worlds — is named the last link, and the whole recital of divine love bends down the scale to reach it. Even failure is treated tenderly: when mortals fail to achieve survival, the paper insists, it is never because the Father's spirit gifts neglected their duty or ministry. And the guardians rise with the guarded — seraphim attain Paradise through serving mortal ascent. Nobody in this universe climbs alone, and nobody who lifts another is left behind for their trouble.
