Who are the celestial artisans?

IAMGODIAM Knowledge Mesh · Urantia Book Study Guides · Paper 44 — The Celestial Artisans · Part II — The Local Universe · reviewed 2026-10-16

The celestial artisans are the master artists and artisans of the morontia and lower spirit realms, the spirits and semispirits engaged in morontia embellishment and spiritual beautification. They are found across the grand universe, but their chief realm of activity is the constellations and the seven hundred seventy worlds around each constellation headquarters. They are not created as artisans: the corps is selected and recruited, and ascending mortals from the evolutionary spheres — beings like us, arrived on the morontia worlds — may apply for admission if sufficiently gifted.

“Though their work may be almost incomprehensible to the material mind, it should be understood that the morontia and spirit worlds are not without their high arts and supernal cultures.”

“Any morontia personality or spirit entity is eligible for admission to the corps of the celestial artisans; that is, any being below the rank of inherent divine sonship. Ascending sons of God from the evolutionary spheres may, after their arrival on the morontia worlds, apply for admission to the artisan corps and, if sufficiently gifted, may choose such a career for a longer or shorter period. But no one may enlist with the celestial artisans for less than one millennium, one thousand years of superuniverse time.”

What this paper teaches

The paper insists the morontia and spirit worlds are not without their high arts and supernal cultures, then walks the seven divisions of artisan work. There are the celestial musicians, whose morontia melodies exceed the limited range of mortal hearing; the heavenly reproducers; the divine builders, for there are cities whose builder and maker is God; the thought recorders, devoted to preserving the superior thought of the realms; the energy manipulators, concerned with physical, mindal, and spiritual energies; and the designers and embellishers, whose exquisite work the author despairs of portraying. Though artisans do not personally work on material planets like Urantia, they come from system headquarters to help naturally gifted individuals.

Why it matters

Here art is not decoration added after the serious work of the universe is done; it is one of the universe's standing professions, staffed in part by former mortals. That dignifies every gifted person whose talent the present world has no use for — the singer, the builder, the keeper of a people's memory. The corps even sets a fair door: any being below inherent divine sonship may apply, and admission turns on giftedness, not birth. For communities whose artistry was stolen, suppressed, or sold, the paper testifies that beautification is heaven's own labor — and that heaven recruits its artists from worlds like this one.
