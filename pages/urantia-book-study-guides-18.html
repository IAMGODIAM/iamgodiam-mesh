What this paper teaches

Geography organizes the paper. Nearest Paradise, seventy Trinitized Secrets of Supremacy direct the seven fraternal spheres of the Father's personal circuit, ruling in corps of ten. Each of the billion worlds of Havona is directed by a personality of this same Trinity origin. Farther out, two hundred and ten Perfections of Days preside over the ten major sectors of each superuniverse, while the Recents of Days — the youngest of the supreme directors — preside in groups of three over the minor sectors. The orders of Days do not administer below the superuniverse level; their lowest reach is the Paradise advisers sent to the rulers of the hundred constellations in each local universe.

Why it matters

Peoples governed by corrupt officials will feel the weight of this paper's central promise: these administrators represent the justice of the Paradise Trinity, and there is never any danger of default or risk of rebellion with Trinity-origin beings — no bribe, no threat, no fatigue moves them off the perfect path. The line of administrative perfection runs unbroken from the Father's own spheres to the capitals of the constellations, which means no province is governed by a lesser standard than the center's. In a world of rigged systems, the paper imagines rule that cannot be purchased — and asks its readers to measure every earthly government against it.
