What is the age of light and life?

IAMGODIAM Knowledge Mesh · Urantia Book Study Guides · Paper 55 — The Spheres of Light and Life · Part II — The Local Universe · reviewed 2026-10-16

The age of light and life is the final evolutionary attainment of a world of time and space — the settled status toward which every inhabited planet is traveling. Paper 55 describes a world that has passed through the ages of the Planetary Prince, the Adams, the Magisterial Sons, and the bestowal Son, and is made ready for its culmination by the successive missions of the Trinity Teacher Sons with their ever-advancing revelations of divine truth and cosmic wisdom.

“This era of light and life, inaugurated by the Teacher Sons at the conclusion of their final planetary mission, continues indefinitely on the inhabited worlds. Each advancing stage of settled status may be segregated by the judicial actions of the Magisterial Sons into a succession of dispensations; but all such judicial actions are purely technical, in no way modifying the course of planetary events.”

What this paper teaches

The paper measures settled existence stage by stage. A morontia temple raised at the planetary capital certifies a world's admission to the settled ages; in that age natural, physical death is no longer a mortal inevitability. The world prospers under the fatherly rule of the Planetary Sovereign, and in successive stages the volunteer Corps of the Finality — ascenders of Paradise attainment — administer its affairs with wise and sympathetic government. Society grows increasingly peaceful. The paper then widens the lens: whole systems settle, then constellations unify under new distributions of authority, and when a universe settles, the Ancients of Days proclaim a supreme council of unlimited authority.

Why it matters

Hold this paper next to the world it names: “a sin-stricken, evil-dominated, self-seeking, isolated world, such as Urantia.” The revelation does not flatter us about the present age — and it refuses to call the present age the end. A world like ours is envisioned with a morontia temple at its capital, death dethroned as an inevitability, and society growing peaceful under just administration. For communities told their neighborhoods are permanent war zones and their children are statistics, this is not fantasy decoration. It is a claim about destiny: broken worlds are not finished worlds, and peace is where the road actually leads.
