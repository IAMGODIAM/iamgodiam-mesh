What are the seven mansion worlds?

IAMGODIAM Knowledge Mesh · Urantia Book Study Guides · Paper 47 — The Seven Mansion Worlds · Part II — The Local Universe · reviewed 2026-10-16

The mansion worlds are the seven satellites of the first transition world encircling Jerusem, the worlds the Creator Son pointed to when he spoke on Urantia of the many mansions in the Father's universe. All fifty-six of Jerusem's encircling worlds serve the transitional culture of ascending mortals, but these seven are specifically the first home of the resurrected dead. There, under morontia supervisors and Melchizedeks, survivors resume their lives and advance world by world through the opening stages of the morontia career.

What this paper teaches

The paper assigns transition world number one itself to the finaliters: it is the headquarters of the finaliter corps assigned to Satania, more than one hundred thousand companies strong, and the site of the infant-receiving schools where salvaged children are raised. On the mansion worlds proper, resurrected survivors resume their lives just where they left off when overtaken by death. The third world is the headquarters of the Mansion World Teachers; on the fourth, pilgrims have well entered the morontia career; transport to the fifth marks a tremendous forward step. On the sixth, sojourners may visit the neighboring transition world to learn of the high spirits of the superuniverse, and the seventh world's experience crowns the immediate postmortal career — after which all Jerusem assembles to welcome the graduates.

Why it matters

Grief asks whether anything of a person's formation survives death, and this paper answers with unusual tenderness: you resume where you left off. Nothing learned, suffered, or loved is treated as waste. Even the smallest losses are provided for — children who die are not misplaced but received and schooled on the finaliter world. And advancement is not solitary: when a class graduates, the whole capital assembles as a committee of welcome. For a people who have buried too many too early, the mansion worlds describe a universe that keeps its dead, teaches them, and celebrates them in public.
