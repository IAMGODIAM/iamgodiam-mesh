Outside Havona there are exactly seven inhabited universes: the superuniverses, vast segments of the grand universe governed from headquarters worlds like Uversa, capital of our own Orvonton. What this paper teaches

The paper is part atlas, part civics lesson. Practically all the starry realms visible to the naked eye on Urantia belong to Orvonton, the seventh superuniverse. Each superuniverse government sits near the center of its segment on a world made to order, and its headquarters sphere doubles as a power-energy regulator for its sectors. The scale is stated plainly: one major sector holds about one hundred billion inhabitable worlds. Government on Uversa has executive and deliberative branches and a judicial branch besides, and there the autocracy of perfection and the democracy of evolution meet face to face. Over the whole structure, seven major purposes are unfolding — and the universal circuits of Paradise pervade every superuniverse.

Why it matters

For a small world far out toward the borderland, the message is belonging. Urantia is not drifting without chart or compass; its local universe pursues a definite, well-understood course, known to star observers the way planetary orbits are known here. And the government over that course is not a distant despotism: on Uversa, perfection and evolving democracy meet face to face, with a judiciary of its own. Peoples who have known only rigged courts and remote capitals are given a different picture of rule — ordered, lawful, and close enough to know every inhabited world by name and number.
