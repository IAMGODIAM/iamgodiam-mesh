Who were Adam and Eve?

IAMGODIAM Knowledge Mesh · Urantia Book Study Guides · Paper 74 — Adam and Eve · Part III — The History of Urantia · reviewed 2026-10-16

Paper 74 introduces the Adam and Eve of Urantia — not the first humans, but a Material Son and Daughter sent as biologic uplifters. They were members of the senior corps of Material Sons on Jerusem, jointly number 14,311, a little more than eight feet tall. For more than fifteen thousand years they had directed the division of experimental energy concerned with modifying living forms, after earlier service teaching new arrivals in Jerusem's citizenship schools. When volunteers were called for the Urantia mission, the entire senior corps volunteered; they were chosen and sent.

What this paper teaches

The paper narrates their arrival: they fell asleep on Jerusem and awakened in the Father's temple on Urantia before a great throng, welcomed first by Van and Amadon, the heroes of the Caligastia secession. Installed as the world's new rulers, they became painfully aware of their planetary isolation. The Melchizedek receivers stayed on duty almost seven years before handing world affairs to Adam. The paper describes the Adamic family grounds of a little over five square miles, the children's training in the 'east of Eden' with four years at the western schools, and explains that the six-days creation story grew from Adam and Eve's six-day survey of the Garden.

Why it matters

Two things dignify this story for a Black-first reading. First, Adam and Eve came as a pair who always serve together and are never separated — uplift in this telling is not a lone hero's errand but shared, household work. Second, they were greeted first not by the powerful but by Van and Amadon, the loyal holdouts who had kept faith through a century of isolation. Heaven's new beginning on Urantia was handed to people who had already proven they would not quit. That pattern — the faithful remnant receiving the future — runs through every liberation story our people tell.
