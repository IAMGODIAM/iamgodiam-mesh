What this paper teaches

The survey descends level by level. The Master Spirits work from executive headquarters on the seven Paradise satellites of the Spirit, through Supreme Executives who co-ordinate the whole post-Havona creation. Below them stand the forty-nine Reflective Spirits of Trinity origin, each attended by Reflective Image Aids — seven on the headquarters of every superuniverse. The Seven Spirits of the Circuits represent the Infinite Spirit and the Master Spirits jointly to Havona's seven circuits. Nearer home, the Creative Spirits share with the Creator Sons in organizing local universes, and a Mother Spirit's sevenfold adjutant bestowal reaches the living creatures of her realm. Together the seven groups form the nucleus of the Third Source and Center's functional family.

Why it matters

What dignifies this paper is where the chain ends. The same system that begins at the personal presence of the Master Spirits on the edge of the eternal Isle runs downward through governments and circuits until it reaches, in the paper's own words, the lowly service of adjutant spirits bestowed on evolutionary mind. Nothing human is beneath the administration's notice. And the supervision is not cold: our own local universe is directed personally by the Creative Mother Spirit resident at its headquarters. Heaven's bureaucracy, it turns out, is a ministry — ordered care that descends until it touches the mind of a single creature on a single world.
