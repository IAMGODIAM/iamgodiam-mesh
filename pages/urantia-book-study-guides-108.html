What do Thought Adjusters come to do?

IAMGODIAM Knowledge Mesh · Urantia Book Study Guides · Paper 108 — Mission and Ministry of Thought Adjusters · Part III — The History of Urantia · reviewed 2026-10-16

They come to be the Universal Father to the mortals of time and space. That is the fundamental work of the divine gifts: to elevate mortal minds and translate immortal souls up to the heights of Paradise perfection, bringing into existence a new kind of being — the eternal union of the perfect Adjuster and the perfected creature. And they volunteer for this service, the paper says, freely and knowingly.

“With these facts before them, it is our belief that the Monitors freely volunteer for assignment. Probably more than one Adjuster volunteers; perhaps the supervising personalized orders select from this group of volunteering Adjusters the one best suited to the task of spiritualizing and eternalizing the personality of the mortal candidate. (In the assignment and service of the Adjusters the sex of the creature is of no consideration.)”

What this paper teaches

The paper follows the Adjusters from dispatch to indwelling. Sent out from Divinington, they are identical in the endowment of existential divinity but vary in experiential quality according to their previous contact with evolutionary creatures. They volunteer as soon as personality forecasts arrive, yet they are not actually assigned until the human subject makes the first moral personality decision — and the choice among volunteers weighs intellectual capacity, spiritual perception, and the strength of character those two powers combined may produce. The Adjusters are organized as an independent working unit administered directly from Divinington, and in the mortal mind they work quite alone. On Urantia, the paper adds, they accept a difficult assignment.

Why it matters

The paper calls it a marvel of divine condescension that exalted and perfect Adjusters offer themselves for actual existence in the minds of material creatures like the mortals of Urantia. Heaven, in this picture, volunteers downward. No one is conscripted into dignity from above; the gift waits, remarkably, for the human side to move first — an Adjuster is not assigned until the child makes a first moral decision. That honors the smallest moral beginnings of the least powerful person on the planet. The combined measure used in their selection is not wealth or station but mind, spirit perception, and the strength of character the two can grow together.
