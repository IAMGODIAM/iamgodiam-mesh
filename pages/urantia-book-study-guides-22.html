Who are the Trinitized Sons of God?

Beyond the descending Sons who come down from Paradise and the ascending mortals who climb up toward it, the revelation names a third family: the Trinitized Sons of God. Some spring from the creative act of perfected beings; others — glorified mortals, seraphim, and midway creatures — earn the embrace of the Paradise Trinity after attaining the center of all things. However they arrive, trinitization marks them permanently: from that moment they are forever devoted to Trinity service and assignment throughout the superuniverses.

What this paper teaches

The paper sorts the trinitized order into three divisions by origin. The Trinitized Sons of Attainment are all Adjuster-fused ascendant mortals who reached Paradise and the Corps of the Finality, then surrendered their finaliter standing at the Trinity embrace; they serve as Mighty Messengers, Those High in Authority, and Those without Name and Number under the Ancients of Days. The Sons of Selection — Custodians and Ambassadors — are recruited from ascendant seraphim, translated midwayers, and Spirit-fused and Son-fused mortals, and are assigned to superuniverse courts. A third company, the creature-trinitized sons, are embraced by the Trinity in classes of seven thousand, and the High Son Assistants crown the presentation.

Why it matters

Consider who actually fills these high offices: men and women who began as mortals on evolutionary worlds, fused with the indwelling Spirit, climbed the long ascent, and were trusted with the government of superuniverses. Even the third Attainment group — beings whose worship grew beyond the skill of all other sons and daughters — carries no name and no number, yet holds exalted rank. For people whose names the world's registers never recorded, that is no small thing. This paper insists that origin is not destiny: the child of a small, troubled world can be embraced by the Trinity itself and seated in the councils of creation.
