Who are the Creator Sons who build and rule the local universes?

The Creator Sons are the makers and rulers of the local universes of time and space, the basic creative units from which the seven superuniverses are built. Each one springs from the union of the Universal Father and the Eternal Son and carries the nature of both parents, yet no two are alike: each is a unique personality, the only-begotten Son of the perfect deity ideal he embodies. Known as Michaels, they go out from Paradise to found their universes, organize them, and reign over them as sovereigns.

What this paper teaches

The paper traces a Creator Son from his origin to his throne. It begins with his dual parentage, flashing into being when the Eternal Son's absolute spiritual ideation meets the Father's absolute personality concept, and describes the primary Paradise Sons as the designers, creators, builders, and administrators of their respective domains. A Creator Son receives the range of his universe only by consent of the Paradise Trinity and confirmation of the supervising Master Spirit. The narrative then classifies the seven groups of bestowal Sons by the number of their creature bestowals, and explains the Master Michael's unquestioned power: unlimited, because it is derived from experienced association with the Trinity and from living as his own creatures.

Why it matters

This paper quietly overturns the way power usually works among us. Earth's rulers commonly inherit office, seize it, or purchase it, and then govern people whose lives they have never lived. A Master Michael holds authority for the opposite reason: he has bestowed himself again and again, experiencing existence as the very creatures subject to his rule, from the highest to the lowest. For communities that have known government without representation for centuries, that pattern carries real weight. It declares that legitimate authority is earned through shared experience, and that the sovereign of a universe is neither distant nor interchangeable — he knows his realm from the inside, because he has been its lowliest citizen.
