What this paper teaches

The paper traces her deepening personhood. From her proceed the established currents and ordained circuits of spirit power destined to pervade every world and being of the universe. At the time of life creation she undergoes a marked personality metamorphosis, and thereafter co-operates in a very personal manner with the Creator Son in planning and managing their creation; she also formulates life with him and creates new orders of beings up to the time of his seventh bestowal. Nebadon has three distinct spirit circuits. As a planet evolves and its inhabitants spiritualize, still more spiritual influences become available to mature personalities — though the flesh, the paper warns, does not naturally bear the fruits of the divine Spirit.

Why it matters

The universe, in this paper, is mothered as truly as it is fathered — and the Mother is no background figure. She is present before the first world is organized, her circuits reach every inhabited sphere, and her personality itself grows through the ages of her work until she is personally recognized by all who contact her. For communities where mothers carry the daily ministry of a people — feeding, teaching, holding life together — and are rarely named as rulers, this paper names the Divine Minister as co-sovereign from the beginning: the pattern may come from the Son, but nothing becomes real until her hands make it so.
