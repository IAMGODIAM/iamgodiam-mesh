Who are the ministering spirits of the central universe?

IAMGODIAM Knowledge Mesh · Urantia Book Study Guides · Paper 26 — Ministering Spirits of the Central Universe · Part I — The Central and Superuniverses · reviewed 2026-10-16

The supernaphim are the ministering spirits of Paradise and the central universe, the highest order of the angelic hosts and the skilled attendants of every being who sojourns there. This paper follows their ministry along the seven circuits of Havona, the long road ascending pilgrims travel toward Paradise. On each circle a different company of these angels takes up the pilgrim: helpers who welcome the traveler, guides of supremacy, Trinity, and the Father, and counselors who prepare the soul for residence on Paradise itself.

“SUPERNAPHIM are the ministering spirits of Paradise and the central universe; they are the highest order of the lowest group of the children of the Infinite Spirit—the angelic hosts. Such ministering spirits are to be encountered from the Isle of Paradise to the worlds of time and space. No major part of the organized and inhabited creation is without their services.”

What this paper teaches

Angels, the paper insists, are not remote ornaments but associates and working colleagues of ascending will creatures, highly individualized personalities with social natures far beyond our own. The supernaphim serve in three orders: the primary, created by the Infinite Spirit; the secondary, offspring of the Master Spirits; and the tertiary, offspring of the Spirits of the Circuits. The secondary order carries the circuit-by-circuit story — pilgrim helpers of quick understanding and broad sympathy on the outer circle, supremacy guides on the sixth, Trinity guides on the fifth, the circuit of the Sons on the fourth, Father guides on the third, superaphic counselors on the second, and final preparation for Paradise residence on the first.

Why it matters

Nobody in this cosmos climbs alone, and nobody is met at the gate by a judge. The first beings a weary pilgrim encounters after the long ascent through space are helpers whose defining gifts are understanding and sympathy — welcome is a celestial profession. Each stage of the road has its own guides, so the help changes as the traveler grows. For peoples whose history is a journey through hostile territory, carried by elders, teachers, and welcomers at every stage, this is a familiar and honored pattern written into the structure of heaven: the road home is staffed, and the staff are kind.
