How do Thought Adjusters grow with the people they indwell?

IAMGODIAM Knowledge Mesh · Urantia Book Study Guides · Paper 109 — Relation of Adjusters to Universe Creatures · Part III — The History of Urantia · reviewed 2026-10-16

They grow up alongside us. The Thought Adjusters are called the children of the universe career: as the personality of the human child expands for the struggles of evolutionary existence, so does the Adjuster wax great in rehearsal for the next stage of ascending life. Human existence, the paper says, is a period of practice which the Adjuster effectively uses to prepare for the greater responsibilities and opportunities of a future life.

“Adjusters pass through a definite developmental career in the mortal mind; they achieve a reality of attainment which is eternally theirs. They progressively acquire Adjuster skill and ability as a result of any and all contacts with the material races, regardless of the survival or nonsurvival of their particular mortal subjects. They are also equal partners of the human mind in fostering the evolution of the immortal soul of survival capacity.”

“The first stage of Adjuster evolution is attained in fusion with the surviving soul of a mortal being. Thus, while you are in nature evolving inward and upward from man to God, the Adjusters are in nature evolving outward and downward from God to man; and so will the final product of this union of divinity and humanity eternally be the son of man and the son of God.”

What this paper teaches

The paper maps the Adjusters' own career among creatures. A comprehensive plan trains virgin Adjusters before they are sent from Divinington, though its details are little known, and Adjusters whose mortal subjects fail of survival receive extended retraining before new missions. By experience they are classified as virgin, advanced, and supreme, with a functional class of self-acting Adjusters besides. Their detailed work varies with assignment, whether liaison or fusion. They greatly help the higher forms of intelligent intercommunication between human beings, and the supreme and self-acting ones can contribute factors of spiritual import when the mind flows freely in the liberated but controlled channels of creative imagination. Above all: Adjusters never fail, and nothing worth surviving is ever lost.

Why it matters

One sentence from this paper's brief deserves to be underlined: actual living experience has no cosmic substitute. Even a perfect fragment of God, this teaching says, cannot be endowed with experienced ability — it must be earned by actually living, in contact with actual people. That is a profound validation of experiential knowledge, the kind carried by people whose wisdom was never credentialed. And its closing assurance is stronger still: Adjusters never fail, and every meaningful value in every will creature is certain of survival. Lives that history failed to record are not lost. What was meaningful in them is kept — by a witness who was there.
