What are the sacred spheres of Paradise?

Between Paradise and the innermost circuit of Havona lie twenty-one special worlds in three circuits of seven. What this paper teaches

The paper takes the three circuits in turn. The Father's seven secret spheres hold the only inherent personality secrets in the universe of universes, and they serve as ultimate status spheres for all Father-endowed personalities — Father fragments are counted among the natives of Divinington, the first of them. The Son's seven luminous worlds are the spheres of the seven phases of pure-spirit existence; no personal being may sojourn there, yet their glory illuminates all Paradise and Havona and directionizes pure spirit luminosity toward the superuniverses. The Spirit's seven orbs are inhabited by his offspring and serve as the executive headquarters from which the Seven Master Spirits preside over the destinies of the seven superuniverses.

Why it matters

Notice the direction of the glory: nothing at the center keeps its light to itself. The Father's spheres reflect divine luminosity outward through Paradise and Havona; the Son's worlds send pure spirit light to the superuniverses; the Spirit's headquarters exist to preside over destinies far from home. Even the secrets here dignify the person — the one inherent personality secret in all creation is kept at the center, as if the inner life of each personality were sacred property. A faith shaped by this paper learns to shine outward the same way: what is received at the center is meant for the edges.
