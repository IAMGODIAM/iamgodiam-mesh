What personalities serve alongside Michael in Nebadon?

This paper surveys the ranks between them and us: the Universe Aids, among them Gabriel, the one Bright and Morning Star in each local universe and its chief executive; the archangels; the Most High Assistants who volunteer from outside the universe; the High Commissioners, who are Spirit-fused ascendant mortals; the Mansion World Teachers, recruited from glorified cherubim; and the orders of permanent citizenship.

What this paper teaches

Many of the Universe Aids remain unrevealed, but seven orders are presented. The Bright and Morning Stars' Evening Star cousins — the Brilliant Evening Stars — were planned by the Melchizedeks and brought into being by the Creator Son and the Creative Spirit, serving chiefly as Gabriel's liaison officers at the capital of every constellation and system. Archangels are offspring of the Son and the Mother Spirit. Nebadon's educational system is jointly administered by the Trinity Teacher Sons and the Melchizedek teaching corps, while higher spirit beings of the Infinite Spirit's family hold permanent assignment in the universe, and permanent citizenship orders anchor its settled life.

Why it matters

Look at who gets trusted with the work: a Commissioner who was once a mortal of the worlds; teachers who were once cherubim, the lowliest of angels, now glorified and instructing the newly risen. The administration of Nebadon is staffed in part by beings who climbed from the bottom of it. That is this paper's quiet promise to everyone who starts low — the ladder is not decorative. Yesterday's student becomes the Mansion World Teacher; yesterday's pilgrim returns as a High Commissioner. Origin is a starting point in Nebadon, never a life sentence.
