What this paper teaches

The Supreme Power Directors were personalized by the Seven Master Spirits, the first recorded derivation of semimaterial offspring from true spirit ancestry; individually the Directors cannot reproduce, but collectively with the Master Spirits they have produced more than ten billion associates. The Supreme Power Centers are distributed throughout the superuniverses, attended by mobile subordinates, while the force organizers reside on Paradise and function chiefly in the domains of unorganized space. The Directors themselves are stationed on peripheral Paradise: singly, each supervises the power-energy affairs of a superuniverse in eternal union with one Master Spirit; collectively, they administer the central creation. Before their day, the Master Force Organizers supervised the energy circuits of space.

Why it matters

The quiet revolution of this paper is that even raw energy is not left to blind machinery. The circuits that carry power across creation are directed by living, accountable beings — infrastructure as a personal trust. Every community knows the dignity and the invisibility of such work: the hands that keep the lights on, the water running, the grid repaired after the storm, noticed only when they fail. Here that labor is lifted to the summit of the physical universe and performed by beings of near-supreme rank. Nothing that sustains life, this paper says, is beneath the care of persons.
