What this paper teaches

The paper surveys the Spirit's equipment for this ministry. He is known by many names of relationship and function, and as God the Spirit he stands as the personality co-ordinate and divine equal of the Father and the Son; some of his attributes derive from the Father, some from the Son, and some appear in neither. Two gifts dominate the survey: energy and mind. His agencies manipulate the forces of all space, and he is infinite in mind — personally conscious of every intellect in creation, maintaining personal and perfect contact with all of them. Finally, he can co-ordinate every level of actuality so that the mental, material, and spiritual are recognized simultaneously, and he freely transmits his powers to subordinate ministering personalities.

Why it matters

The most quoted line in this paper deserves its fame: the Spirit's influence and personalities “really know and truly understand you.” Ministry, here, is not surveillance from above; it is knowledge that understands. And the scope of that attention abolishes insignificance: the Third Source is personally conscious of every mind, every intellect, in all creation — there is no mind too small, too poor, or too far from the centers of power to be held in perfect contact. He also transmits his own powers and prerogatives downward to subordinate personalities. In this universe, even God shares power rather than hoarding it.
