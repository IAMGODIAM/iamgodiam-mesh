What this paper teaches

The paper moves level by level. The primary phases of finite reality appear on creature levels as perfect personalities and on universe levels as the perfect Havona creation. God the Sevenfold functionally co-ordinates finite evolution, while the Supreme Being eventually synthesizes destiny attainment: the absonite architects eventuate the plan, the Supreme Creators bring it into existence, and the Supreme will consummate its fullness. The Paradise Trinity co-ordinates in the ultimate sense, and the Ultimate stands as the apex of transcendental reality, even as the Supreme is the capstone of the evolutionary-experiential. Yet the master universe's present potential is hardly absolute, and the paper ends by theorizing the Trinity of Trinities — a possible experiential unification of limitless reality in the far remoteness of eternity.

Why it matters

The opening sentence of this paper is a study program for any people trying to find their bearings: know the levels of reality and your place among them, and your earthly orientation, cosmic insight, and spiritual direction all improve. Communities that have been located by other people's maps — named, zoned, and written off — know why that matters. The paper's own categories carry hope: even a maximum, seemingly final in the present age, may be no more than a real beginning in the ages to come. What looks like the ceiling of a people's story, measured from inside this age, is not the last level of reality.
