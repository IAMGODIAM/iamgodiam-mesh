Who are the Higher Personalities of the Infinite Spirit?

IAMGODIAM Knowledge Mesh · Urantia Book Study Guides · Paper 24 — Higher Personalities of the Infinite Spirit · Part I — The Central and Superuniverses · reviewed 2026-10-16

On Uversa, the capital of our superuniverse, everything created by the Conjoint Creator is arranged in three grand divisions, and the Higher Personalities of the Infinite Spirit stand first. This senior family includes the Solitary Messengers, the Universe Circuit Supervisors who oversee the circuits of energy and spirit, the Personal Aids of the Conjoint Creator, the Seven Supreme Executives who administer the superuniverses, their liaison Assigned Sentinels, and the Graduate Guides, who conduct the great university of training that prepares ascending mortals for eternity.

“ON UVERSA we divide all personalities and entities of the Conjoint Creator into three grand divisions: the Higher Personalities of the Infinite Spirit, the Messenger Hosts of Space, and the Ministering Spirits of Time, those spirit beings who are concerned with teaching and ministering to the will creatures of the ascendant scheme of mortal progression.”

What this paper teaches

The paper works through the family order by order. The vast power currents and spirit-energy circuits of space only appear to run automatically; in fact they are personally supervised, down to a tertiary circuit supervisor stationed on the headquarters world of every local universe. Of the Personal Aids, even the revelators confess no authentic knowledge of when or how they were created. The Seven Supreme Executives function collectively as the administrative board of supermanagers for the seven superuniverses, and the Assigned Sentinels carry their coordination into every realm. The presentation closes with the Graduate Guides, sponsors of the high university of technical instruction and spiritual training essential to mortal attainment, believed to be perfected Havona Servitals.

Why it matters

The deepest claim here is that the universe is personal all the way up. The currents that look like blind machinery are supervised; the administration of seven superuniverses is a board of persons; and among the highest offices in creation sits an order devoted entirely to teaching — the Graduate Guides, whose whole work is schooling ascending mortals for the goal of the ages. For a people long denied schools and then blamed for ignorance, there is dignity in this architecture: heaven's executive branch treats the education of small-world pilgrims as first-order business, staffed by its most perfected servants.
