Who are the Messenger Hosts of Space?

The Messenger Hosts of Space hold the middle rank in the family of the Infinite Spirit, serving as the connecting links between the higher personalities above them and the ministering spirits below. The paper presents their orders in turn: the Havona Servitals of the central universe, the Universal Conciliators, the Technical Advisers, the Custodians of Records and the recorders who duplicate every archive, the Morontia Companions who befriend ascending pilgrims, and the Paradise Companions, a composite company gathered from many angelic ranks.

What this paper teaches

Only three of the seven groups — servitals, conciliators, and Morontia Companions — are created as such; the other four are attainment levels reached by the angelic orders, so this middle family is largely a family of promotion. The Havona Servitals are the joint creative work of the Seven Master Spirits and the Seven Supreme Power Directors, and for every servital created, seven Universal Conciliators come into being, one for each superuniverse, maintaining their headquarters and reserve corps on the superuniverse capital. The Technical Advisers, the legal and technical minds of the spirit world, were not created in that role but grew into it. The Custodians guard the formal archives of the Isle of Light, while other recorders execute every record in duplicate, a spirit original and a semimaterial copy.

Why it matters

One sentence in this paper deserves to be read aloud wherever work is divided into the honored and the humble: in the spiritual world there is no such thing as menial work, and the higher orders do not look down on the lower. The hosts honored here are precisely the connectors — conciliators who reconcile, companions who befriend travelers, recorders who keep the memory of deeds in duplicate so nothing is lost. Communities survive on exactly this labor: mediation, companionship, and the keeping of records the powerful would rather forget. This paper seats that work in heaven's middle court and calls it sacred.
