What this paper teaches

The paper traces the chain of supervision. Urantia's original sovereignty was held in trust by the sovereign of the Satania system; since Michael's bestowal, general management rests with a special group on Jerusem of twenty-four onetime Urantians, who every century designate one of their number as resident governor general on the world. It notes the complication of Norlatiadek's onetime seizure of planetary authority after the rebellion, describes the twelve corps of special seraphim who arrived with the first governor general at the outpouring of the Spirit of Truth, and closes with the reserve corps of destiny — living men and women admitted to the special service of the superhuman administration.

Why it matters

The most startling line in this paper is its last idea: the reserve corps of destiny is made of living men and women. Heaven's administration of this world is not staffed only by angels; ordinary mortals are admitted to its special service. For people who feel governed entirely by powers that despise them — distant capitals, rigged markets, occupying systems — the paper insists the true government of the world includes neighbors quietly enlisted for its welfare. History, it says, has more authors than the ones on the throne. That hidden roster is a summons to take one's post seriously.
