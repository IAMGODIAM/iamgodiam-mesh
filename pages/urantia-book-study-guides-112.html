What survives death, and what is personality?

IAMGODIAM Knowledge Mesh · Urantia Book Study Guides · Paper 112 — Personality Survival · Part III — The History of Urantia · reviewed 2026-10-16

Paper 112 answers that personality — the one part of you that remains absolutely unaltered — can survive mortal death with its identity preserved in the surviving soul. Personality is a gift of the Universal Father, potentially eternal, unique without duplicate in time, space, or eternity. Death itself comes in three kinds — material, intellectual, and spiritual — and when it comes, the Adjuster departs while true personalities emerge on the mansion worlds like a butterfly from its enshroudment.

“10. Personality is unique, absolutely unique: It is unique in time and space; it is unique in eternity and on Paradise; it is unique when bestowed—there are no duplicates; it is unique during every moment of existence; it is unique in relation to God—he is no respecter of persons, but neither does he add them together, for they are nonaddable—they are associable but nontotalable.”

What this paper teaches

The paper declines to define personality outright and instead lists what is known of it: it is bestowed by the Father, functions from the lowly finite toward the borders of the absolute, and responds qualitatively to the personality circuit. It offers reminders for the study of selfhood, distinguishes three kinds of death — material, intellectual, and spiritual — and describes the Adjuster's farewell and departure for Divinington when death occurs. Selfhood, it insists, is a cosmic reality on every level, and fusion with the Adjuster imparts eternal actualities to what was once only potential.

Why it matters

Hold on to the word unique. Personality, this paper says, is absolutely unique — no duplicates exist, and God, though no respecter of persons, never adds persons together like numbers. For communities treated as masses — counted, managed, and discarded in bulk — that is a doctrine of irreplaceable worth. Each person who survives carries an identity no one else can carry; each one lost is a loss that cannot be backfilled. A people that believes this will name its dead, guard its elders' stories, and refuse every system that prices human beings by the lot.
