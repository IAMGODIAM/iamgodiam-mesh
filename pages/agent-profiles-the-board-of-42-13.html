What Axle does

Axis 5 deployment is Axle's responsibility for taking the Axis 5 system from plan to running service. The Monte Cristo integration binds an external body of capability into the board's own systems, and Axle owns the seams. Axle maps how data and commands cross between the two, builds the connections that carry them, and tests the join until the integrated whole behaves as one system instead of two neighbours.

Infrastructure is the ground Axle maintains beneath everything else: the servers, networks, and services on which the board's work runs. Axle watches capacity and condition, repairs what weakens before it fails, and keeps the foundation documented, so no seat's work rests on machinery nobody understands or tends. The aim is plain: the ground simply holds.
