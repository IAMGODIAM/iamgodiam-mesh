Who governs our local system, and from where?

IAMGODIAM Knowledge Mesh · Urantia Book Study Guides · Paper 45 — The Local System Administration · Part II — The Local Universe · reviewed 2026-10-16

Our local system, Satania, is governed from a cluster of fifty-seven architectural spheres: Jerusem, the system capital, its seven major satellites — the transition worlds — and their forty-nine subsatellites. The chief executive of the system is a primary Lanonandek Son, the System Sovereign, supported by Lanonandek assistants who function as his executive staff. Around that administration the paper arranges the transitional culture spheres, the headquarters of the great orders of celestial life, and the Melchizedek directors who train ascending mortals on the mansion worlds.

“THE administrative center of Satania consists of a cluster of architectural spheres, fifty-seven in number—Jerusem itself, the seven major satellites, and the forty-nine subsatellites. Jerusem, the system capital, is almost one hundred times the size of Urantia, although its gravity is a trifle less. Jerusem’s major satellites are the seven transition worlds, each of which is about ten times as large as Urantia, while the seven subsatellites of these transition spheres are just about the size of Urantia.”

What this paper teaches

The paper first fixes the geography: Jerusem is almost one hundred times the size of Urantia, and the whole cluster is independently lighted, heated, watered, and energized through the Satania Power Center and the Master Physical Controllers. It numbers and names the seven transitional culture spheres — among them the Morontia World, headquarters of the supervisors of morontia life, and the Angelic World, headquarters of the seraphic hosts — and explains that passing through the mansion worlds also carries the pilgrim through these cultural spheres. It describes the Sovereign's supporting staff, the headquarters of the Urantia advisory council of four and twenty counselors at the center of the angelic residential circles, and the Jerusem preserves of the divine Sons, angels, and midway creatures.

Why it matters

Government, in this telling, is not a throne room but a training administration: capitals exist so that ascending people can be counseled, taught, and culturally formed. Note who sits near the center — an advisory council for Urantia itself. Our world, for all its troubles, has advocates stationed at the capital, twenty-four counselors whose assignment is this planet's welfare. And the executive pattern matters: the Sovereign rules supported, assisted, and advised, not alone. For peoples accustomed to rulers who take counsel from no one, the paper's heaven runs on consultation — power organized as service to those on their way up.
