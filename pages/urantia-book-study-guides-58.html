How was life planted on Urantia?

Urantia is a decimal planet — a life-modification world, one of only sixty-one in all Satania — where the Life Carriers are permitted to experiment in improving the standard types of living beings. Paper 58 tells how their commission arrived from Jerusem six hundred million years ago, studied the physical conditions of world number 606, and prepared to launch life in a sodium chloride pattern once the oceans were briny enough to sustain it.

“IN ALL Satania there are only sixty-one worlds similar to Urantia, life-modification planets. But about one world in ten is designated as a decimal planet and assigned to the special registry of the Life Carriers; and on such planets we are permitted to undertake certain life experiments in an effort to modify or possibly improve the standard universe types of living beings.”

What this paper teaches

The paper follows the planting carefully. Life Carriers cannot initiate life until a sphere is ripe, nor speed development beyond what the planet's physical progress can support. The atmosphere's filtering of sunlight, the hydrogen clouds of the early world, and the slow preparation of the stage all had to precede the first marine forms. The paper explains who the Life Carriers are, traces the continuing continental drift, dates the transition from vegetable to animal life at 450,000,000 years ago, and notes that the Proterozoic rock systems of the life-dawn era no longer appear at many points on the earth's surface.

Why it matters

Nothing here is rushed, and nothing is accidental. The gardeners of this world studied it for ages, waited for the seas to turn briny, and planted life fitted to the place — and even now, the text says, the same salt sea circulates in our blood, bathing every cell. Our bodies carry the ocean we came from. For a people carried across an ocean in chains and yet not extinguished, that image lands with force: what was planted in us by creation itself still circulates, cell by cell, and no voyage and no violence has been able to wash it out.
