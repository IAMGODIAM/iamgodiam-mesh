Who runs a local universe day to day?

IAMGODIAM Knowledge Mesh · Urantia Book Study Guides · Paper 33 — Administration of the Local Universe · Part II — The Local Universe · reviewed 2026-10-16

The Universal Father rules his vast creation, but in a local universe he works through the Creator Son. Day-to-day administration belongs to Michael of Nebadon and the local universe Mother Spirit, and through them to their manifold children: Gabriel holds executive power as the actual administrator of Nebadon, while jurisdictional authority runs down through the Constellation Fathers, System Sovereigns, and Planetary Princes. Michael himself is supremely concerned with three things only — creation, sustenance, and ministry — and on Salvington, the headquarters world, there are no true legislative bodies at all.

“WHILE the Universal Father most certainly rules over his vast creation, he functions in a local universe administration through the person of the Creator Son. The Father does not otherwise personally function in the administrative affairs of a local universe. These matters are intrusted to the Creator Son and to the local universe Mother Spirit and to their manifold children. The plans, policies, and administrative acts of the local universe are formed and executed by this Son, who, in conjunction with his Spirit associate, delegates executive power to Gabriel and jurisdictional authority to the Constellation Fathers, System Sovereigns, and Planetary Princes.”

What this paper teaches

The paper introduces our Creator Son as the personification of the 611,121st original concept of infinite identity arising simultaneously in the Father and the Eternal Son — a Son who, having lived all three phases of creature existence (spiritual, morontial, and material), is sometimes called Christ Michael after his seventh bestowal on Urantia. Some Creator Sons resemble the Father more, others the Son; ours manifests traits that more resemble the Eternal Son. The Infinite Spirit, pervading all universes, focalizes at each local universe headquarters and acquires full personality qualities there; the Bright and Morning Star, Gabriel, is the personalization of the first joint concept of the Son and the Spirit. Trinity-origin administration ends with the superuniverses — below that, the Son and Spirit govern.

Why it matters

Consider what the sovereign refuses to do: he does not legislate endlessly, and he does not rule from absence. The one who governs Nebadon has himself lived the material life of his creatures, and the paper says plainly that in him we have a ruler just as mighty, efficient, and beneficent as the Father would be if personally present. Authority here is delegation with real power attached — Gabriel truly administers; princes truly hold jurisdiction — and the whole structure leans toward sustenance and ministry rather than control. It is a picture of government as parenthood, exercised close to home.
