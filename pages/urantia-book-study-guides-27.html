What do the primary supernaphim do on Paradise?

IAMGODIAM Knowledge Mesh · Urantia Book Study Guides · Paper 27 — Ministry of the Primary Supernaphim · Part I — The Central and Superuniverses · reviewed 2026-10-16

The primary supernaphim are the supernal servants of the Deities on the eternal Isle of Paradise — perfect beings of whom, from eternity, not one has ever been lost. Ascending pilgrims do not come under their direct influence until actually attaining Paradise residence, and then these angels conduct a final training in seven orders of service. The pilgrim enters under the instigators of rest, passes through the intervening orders, and finishes with the conductors of worship, ready to begin the endless career of a finaliter.

What this paper teaches

The seven services trace a whole curriculum of eternity. The instigators of rest, inspectors of Paradise, collaborate with the complements of rest on Havona's inner circuit to prepare pilgrims for the last metamorphic sleep, the transition out of time and space into residential status on Paradise. One designated company, presided over by the chief supernaphim called the original pattern angel, organizes all three angelic orders. Then come the conductors of ethics — the higher the ascent, the more attention universe ethics demand — followed by instruction in the inherent proprieties of Paradise relationships, the custodians of knowledge who are living epistles read by all, the masters of philosophy, and finally the conductors of worship.

Why it matters

Look at what heaven teaches last and first. The pilgrim's final instructors before eternity begin with rest — divine rest, treated not as collapse after labor but as a sacred discipline taught by the highest angels on the innermost shore. Only after rest come ethics, conduct, knowledge, philosophy, and worship, named the highest privilege and first duty of every created intelligence. For an exhausted people, worked for centuries as if rest were theft, this curriculum is a verdict in their favor: the universe itself ends the long journey with rest, and crowns the rested mind with philosophy and praise.
