Who plants life on new worlds?

IAMGODIAM Knowledge Mesh · Urantia Book Study Guides · Paper 36 — The Life Carriers · Part II — The Local Universe · reviewed 2026-10-16

Life does not originate spontaneously. On the worlds of Nebadon it is designed, carried, and planted by the Life Carriers — a peculiar and distinct order of universe Sons, the only group in whose creation the rulers of a superuniverse participate: the Creator Son, the Universe Mother Spirit, and a designated Ancient of Days. Nebadon records the creation of one hundred million of them. After planting life on a new world they do not leave; they remain there for long periods to foster its development, and the seven adjutant mind-spirits on those primitive worlds make evolution purposeful rather than accidental.

“LIFE does not originate spontaneously. Life is constructed according to plans formulated by the (unrevealed) Architects of Being and appears on the inhabited planets either by direct importation or as a result of the operations of the Life Carriers of the local universes. These carriers of life are among the most interesting and versatile of the diverse family of universe Sons. They are intrusted with designing and carrying creature life to the planetary spheres. And after planting this life on such new worlds, they remain there for long periods to foster its development.”

What this paper teaches

The Life Carriers work under a directing trio — Gabriel, the Father Melchizedek, and Nambia, the original and first-born Life Carrier of Nebadon — though in their divisional administration they are self-governing. They are graded into three grand divisions, senior carriers, assistants, and custodians, sorted by tests the Melchizedeks conducted on their headquarters sphere; the Melchizedeks keep general oversight of the Life Carriers' worlds in the Salvington circuit, and in every local system there is a single sphere where Melchizedeks themselves once functioned as life carriers. The paper closes by holding two truths together: life is both mechanistic and vitalistic, material and spiritual — and when a world is finally settled in light and life, its Life Carriers take seats in its higher deliberative bodies.

Why it matters

Every inhabited world in this account was worth designing for, worth the journey, and worth staying with. Life is not an accident that happened to matter; it is a trust delivered by carriers who remain through the long fosterage of a planet's development, under overseers who themselves once did the carrying. For people told that their beginnings were random or their communities disposable, the paper answers with intention: somebody planned this life, somebody carried it here, and somebody stayed. Evolution with companions is a very different story from evolution alone.
