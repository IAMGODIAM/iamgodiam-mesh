What holds a local universe together physically?

IAMGODIAM Knowledge Mesh · Urantia Book Study Guides · Paper 41 — Physical Aspects of the Local Universe · Part II — The Local Universe · reviewed 2026-10-16

Paper 41 describes the physical machinery of Nebadon, our local universe. What marks one local creation off from all others is the space presence of the Creative Spirit; within her borders, power centers and Master Physical Controllers organize and channel the energies of space. The paper traces how nebulae give origin to suns and systems, how the suns pour forth light and energy, and how solar energy is generated and governed — a universe held together not by accident, but by continuous, administered upholding.

“THE characteristic space phenomenon which sets off each local creation from all others is the presence of the Creative Spirit. All Nebadon is certainly pervaded by the space presence of the Divine Minister of Salvington, and such presence just as certainly terminates at the outer borders of our local universe. That which is pervaded by our local universe Mother Spirit is Nebadon; that which extends beyond her space presence is outside Nebadon, being the extra-Nebadon space regions of the superuniverse of Orvonton—other local universes.”

What this paper teaches

The paper opens with the Creative Spirit's space presence as the boundary of Nebadon, then moves to origins: spiral and other nebulae, initiated by Paradise force organizers, become the mother wheels of the spheres. It explains the Master Physical Controllers at work in a local system such as Satania, and turns to the suns — upward of two thousand brilliant suns in Satania, our own an average orb — their mass, their surprising lack of density, and the steady escape of light-energies. Spectral phenomena warn that space is not empty; the paper closes with solar interiors, nuclear-reaction chains like the hydrogen-carbon-helium reaction, gravity's grip on electrons, and variable stars giving birth to new systems.

Why it matters

A people told daily that the world runs on blind force can hear something else here: in this account, energy is administered by living presences, and even the sunlight is upheld, channeled, and governed with intention. The same sun that shines on Satania's two thousand worlds shines on ours — no corner of the creation is off the grid of care. Studying the physical order becomes an act of reverence rather than fear: the universe is not a machine that forgot its makers, and the small worlds inside it, including this one, are held.
