What this paper teaches

The paper is frank about its limits: since Adjusters are of the essence of original Deity, no one may presume to discourse authoritatively on their nature and origin; the author can only impart the traditions of Salvington and the beliefs of Uversa. Adjusters are individuated as virgin entities, each destined to become a liberated, fused, or Personalized Monitor, and all universe activities of their dispatch, management, direction, and return center on the sacred sphere of Divinington. To call an Adjuster divine is simply to recognize that origin. Adjusters are not personalities, the paper says, but they are real entities — truly and perfectly individualized — and wherever they are encountered in creature experience, they disclose the presence and leading of a spirit influence.

Why it matters

Sit with what this paper claims: the eternal Father, resident at the center of all things, is also actually present in the minds of his countless children of time — not the important ones, not the credentialed ones, all of them. Every doctrine that has ever ranked human beings by the presence or absence of a soul breaks on this sentence. The person the world calls nobody carries, in this teaching, the same divine fragment as the person it calls somebody, and that fragment is the unquenchable yearning to be like God. Dignity here is not conferred by recognition. It is indwelt, and it cannot be zoned away.
