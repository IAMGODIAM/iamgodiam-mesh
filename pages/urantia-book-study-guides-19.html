What this paper teaches

The paper's groups divide by function. The Trinity Teacher Sons, alone among high celestial personalities, act in a dual capacity, and their number keeps growing — the last periodic report to Uversa counted 21,001,624,821 in service — while most of the other groups are of definite, finished numbers. The Perfectors of Wisdom personify the wisdom of divinity in the superuniverses; another order serves as the counsel of Deity to those same realms; and there are exactly eight billion Universal Censors in existence. About the Inspired Trinity Spirits the paper confesses it can say very little: they are one of the few wholly secret orders of beings. The survey closes with the Havona natives, direct creations of the Trinity beyond counting, and the Paradise Citizens.

Why it matters

The Teacher Sons carry this paper's sharpest word for our moment: the great cosmic danger is the circumscribed viewpoint — the evil of seeing reality in segments and mistaking the segment for the whole. Against that error, heaven stations teachers: liaison beings whose whole work is bridging gulfs between kinds of personalities. A reader formed by communities that were themselves segmented — redlined, partitioned, told their fragment was their worth — can hear the dignity in it. Wisdom, counsel, and judgment all exist in this paper, but teaching leads, because a universe held together must first be seen whole.
