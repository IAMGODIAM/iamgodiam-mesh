Who are the midway creatures?

IAMGODIAM Knowledge Mesh · Urantia Book Study Guides · Paper 77 — The Midway Creatures · Part III — The History of Urantia · reviewed 2026-10-16

Paper 77 introduces the midway creatures — beings who function on a level about midway between the mortals of the realms and the angelic orders. They appear to be an accident of time, the paper says, yet they occur so widely and are so valuable as helpers that they are accepted as an essential order of the combined planetary ministry. On Urantia two distinct orders serve: the primary or senior corps, from the days of Dalamatia, and the secondary or younger group, dating from the times of Adam.

“MOST of the inhabited worlds of Nebadon harbor one or more groups of unique beings existing on a life-functioning level about midway between those of the mortals of the realms and of the angelic orders; hence are they called midway creatures. They appear to be an accident of time, but they occur so widespreadly and are so valuable as helpers that we have all long since accepted them as one of the essential orders of our combined planetary ministry.”

What this paper teaches

The paper traces both orders' origins. The primary midwayers arose from a unique interassociation of the material and the spiritual on Urantia, produced by the Prince's corporeal staff — first fifty, then a corps of 50,000. The secondary midwayers descend from the Adamic side of the story, offspring of pure Adamic stock united with humanized descendants. The paper pauses over the Nodite antecedents, including Dalamatia's submergence and the dispersion after Babel. Most primary midwayers went into sin at the Lucifer rebellion; yet at the world's last adjudication the midwayers were left behind to assist in the planet's spiritual and semispiritual work, the first permanent inhabitants of worlds like ours.

Why it matters

There is comfort and accountability in a midway ministry. Comfort, because this world has never been left unattended: helpers stand permanently between earth and heaven, closer to mortal life than angels, and they stayed when others fell or departed. Accountability, because the midwayers are this planet's own — born of its peoples and its history, not imported. Liberation traditions have always known such figures: the unseen ones who carry messages, guard the camp, and remember what the people forget. The paper asks us to imagine our world as accompanied, and our struggles as witnessed by those assigned to stay.
