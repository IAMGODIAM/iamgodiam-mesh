What this paper teaches

The paper begins before man: the prehuman era runs from life initiation until human beings appear, closing with man's emergence from the animal level, when he can choose to worship the Creator. The Planetary Prince's arrival opens the second epoch, and when evolutionary life has run its biologic course, the second order of sonship — the Adamic pair — begins a new dispensation of grace. On normal worlds the next age opens with the races blended and biologically fit, and when a standard of intellectual and spiritual development is attained, a Paradise bestowal Son always arrives — the Prince of Peace. The final epoch belongs to the Trinity Teacher Sons.

Why it matters

This long calendar carries a word for peoples told they are behind, primitive, or forgotten. In the paper's own telling, primitive man is not a lesser human — he is man at the moment he can choose to worship, already receptive to the indwelling of the divine Adjusters and sealed for survival. Every epoch is a real age with its own dignity, and no age is the whole story. History, on this account, is not a ladder some races climbed and others missed; it is one ascent, staged across ages, and every generation stands somewhere on the stairs by design.
