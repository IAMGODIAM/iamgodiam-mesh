Hermie Agent — Privacy Policy

How the Hermie Agent application accesses, uses, stores and discloses Google user data.

Effective 28 August 2026 · Last updated 28 August 2026

What Hermie Agent is

Hermie Agent is an internal automation tool built and operated by E5 Enclave Incorporated, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization based in Miami, Florida. It manages E5 Enclave's own digital marketing infrastructure: our Google Ads campaigns, our sitemaps and search indexing, and our web analytics configuration.

It is used only by authorized E5 Enclave personnel, acting on E5 Enclave's own Google accounts and web properties. It is not a consumer product, it is not offered to the public, and it does not process data belonging to anyone outside our organization.

Google user data we access

When an authorized member of our team signs in, Hermie Agent requests the following OAuth scopes. Each is listed with the specific reason we need it.

ScopeWhat it permitsWhy we need it
auth/adwordsRead and manage Google Ads accountsTo audit campaign performance, configure conversion tracking, and set bidding strategy on E5 Enclave's own Google Ad Grant account.
auth/webmastersRead and manage Search Console propertiesTo submit sitemaps and monitor indexing for websites E5 Enclave owns.
auth/siteverificationVerify ownership of websitesTo prove E5 Enclave controls its own domains so those properties can be managed.
auth/analytics.editEdit Google Analytics configurationTo define conversion events on E5 Enclave's own Analytics properties.

We do not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Contacts, Calendar, Photos, or any other Google service. We do not read personal messages, files, or contact lists.

How we use the data

Data retrieved through these scopes is used for one purpose only: operating E5 Enclave's own marketing and web properties. Concretely, that means reading campaign and search performance figures, writing configuration changes to our own accounts, and producing internal reports for our board and staff.

We do not use Google user data to train machine-learning or generalized artificial-intelligence models.

Limited Use disclosure

Hermie Agent's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Specifically: we do not sell Google user data; we do not transfer it to third parties except as necessary to provide or improve this tool, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger or acquisition; we do not use it for advertising directed at anyone other than E5 Enclave's own campaigns; and we do not allow humans to read it except with the account holder's consent, for security purposes, to comply with applicable law, or where the data has been aggregated and anonymized.

Storage and security

  • OAuth refresh and access tokens are stored on E5 Enclave-controlled systems with filesystem permissions restricted to the operating account.
  • Tokens are never written to logs, transmitted in URLs, or shared over chat or email.
  • Reports generated from Google data are stored in E5 Enclave's private, access-controlled repositories.
  • Access is limited to authorized E5 Enclave officers and staff.

Retention and deletion

We retain campaign and search performance data only as long as it is useful for operating and reporting on our programs. Authorization can be revoked at any time by the account holder at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately ends this application's access.

To request deletion of data associated with your Google account, contact us at the address below and we will act on the request without undue delay.

Sharing

We do not sell data. We do not share Google user data with third parties for their own purposes. Infrastructure providers that process data on our behalf — including Google Cloud and Cloudflare — act as service providers under our instruction and are bound by their own agreements with us.

Children

Hermie Agent is an internal operations tool for adult staff of E5 Enclave Incorporated. It is not directed at children and is not made available to anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we materially change how this application handles Google user data, we will update this page and revise the "last updated" date above.
